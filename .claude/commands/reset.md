description: Throw away unpublished changes and start again from the staging version
---

Discard everything not yet published. **This destroys work permanently.**

## Always confirm first, and be specific

List what will be lost, in their words:

> This will throw away:
> • the new headline on the About page
> • the two changed paragraphs on /solutions/discovery
>
> These have not been published, so there's no copy of them anywhere. Once
> they're gone I can't get them back.
>
> Are you sure? (yes / no)

**Do not proceed on anything but a clear yes.** If they hesitate, offer to
publish first instead — then it is recoverable with `/undo`.

## Then

Discard local changes and return to the published state of `staging`, then
confirm:

> Done. You're back to what's on the staging site. Nothing published was
> affected.

**Never** reset in a way that discards published work, and never touch `main`.
